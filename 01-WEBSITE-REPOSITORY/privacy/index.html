For younger players

Your privacy, in simple words

Use a nickname. Don’t enter your address, school, phone number or other private details in a bluff or screenshot.

Your game stays on the device you share with the other players. They may be able to see it. We don’t receive your game answers through an online game service.

If you email us, we can see your email address and message. A trusted adult can help you contact us, and you can also ask us a privacy question yourself.

What stays on your device

The game handles player names or nicknames, question mode, Little Fibber Help selections, invented answers, votes, scores, game progress, timer settings and sound choices on your device. It does not ask for age or date of birth.

Names, answers, votes and game progress are held in memory for the active game. The current release does not save this gameplay for recovery after the app closes. There is no player-account service or FABRICATE! cloud synchronisation, and this information is not sent to a FABRICATE! game server.

The sound-effects preference is saved in browser or app local storage until changed or that storage is cleared. Background music starts off on each fresh opening and must be switched on for that session. Data left by older versions may remain until site or app data is cleared.

To leave and clear an active game, open the Exit button during play and confirm. This does not remove your sound-effects choice. To remove browser storage, clear this website’s site data in browser settings. For an installed app, use applicable device app-data/removal controls. We cannot remotely access, recover or erase information held only on your device.

Cookies, local storage and tracking

The current game uses local storage for your sound-effects preference. Its own code does not set cookies or include analytics, advertising trackers, ad SDKs, social-media embeds or third-party fonts.

These Privacy, Terms and Support pages contain no scripts, forms or browser-storage code. Their styling is included in the page files, and the FABRICATE artwork is loaded from the bundled website assets.

Loading website files sends normal web requests to the host. The game’s music and sound clips are included in its files; they do not upload your answers or scores. Opening an external link contacts that service and is subject to its privacy practices.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. Normal requests also disclose technical request information to the hosting service.

GitHub’s processing, security retention and international-transfer arrangements are explained in its Privacy Statement. We do not operate a separate visitor analytics service in this version.

If you email support

When you email support@bluffle.co.uk, we receive your email address, message, attachments and normal email delivery information. We use this to answer your enquiry, investigate issues, respond to privacy requests and maintain necessary records. You do not need to email us to play.

Mark Atkin is the only person handling FABRICATE! support messages. The mailbox is supplied through GoDaddy using Microsoft’s Outlook email service. GoDaddy, Microsoft and service providers involved in delivering, securing and storing email process information as part of providing those services.

Our lawful basis for routine support is legitimate interests: providing help you request, maintaining a functioning game and protecting users. We limit the information used to what is needed, taking particular care with children’s information. Where we must retain or disclose information to meet a legal obligation, that obligation is the lawful basis.

We do not use support messages for marketing or sell them. Please remove personal information from screenshots and never send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary information about children.

How long support messages are kept

Routine support correspondence is kept while an enquiry is open and for up to 12 months after it closes, then deleted or anonymised. This is our support-retention policy, not a legally prescribed period.

Relevant information may be retained longer where necessary to meet a specific legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. We keep only what is needed, record the reason and review whether it is still required.

Deleted messages may remain temporarily in provider recovery systems or backups under the provider’s deletion and retention arrangements. Local game storage and GitHub’s security records have the separate retention arrangements described above.

Service providers and overseas processing

Hosting and email providers operate internationally, so technical website information and support correspondence may be processed outside the UK, including in the United States.

GitHub, GoDaddy and Microsoft describe international-transfer protections in their privacy information and applicable data-protection terms. Depending on the service and destination, these include recognised adequacy arrangements and standard contractual safeguards, with UK-specific terms where required. This notice does not promise UK-only storage.

See the GoDaddy Privacy Policy and data-processing terms, Microsoft Privacy Statement, and Microsoft data-protection terms. You can contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your correspondence or how to obtain a copy.

We may disclose relevant information when legally required or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Links to external sites open services with their own privacy practices.

Your privacy rights

You can ask for access to personal information we hold, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and, where applicable, portability. Rights depend on the circumstances and have legal limits. Contact support@bluffle.co.uk; there is normally no charge, and we normally respond within one month, subject to lawful exceptions or extensions.

Your right to object: you can object to our use of your information based on legitimate interests. Tell us your circumstances and we will consider the request and explain our response.

We may ask for proportionate information to confirm we are dealing with the correct person. Children have privacy rights too; we can explain them in accessible language and assist an appropriate representative.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). You do not have to contact us first. Data kept only on your own device must be removed using that device’s controls.

Purchases, TestFlight and future changes

FABRICATE is free to download. The optional Premium upgrade in the iPhone and iPad app is a one-time Apple in-app purchase. Apple processes payment and keeps its purchase records under its own privacy terms. We do not receive payment-card details. On your device, the app uses Apple-verified purchase entitlement information to enable Premium and Restore Purchases. The Premium question bank is included in the app and supplied to gameplay after entitlement is verified. We do not upload gameplay or purchase receipts to our own server.

If you test through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares testing information with the developer. This can include your name or email address and feedback or screenshots you choose to submit. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the game, based on our legitimate interests in testing and maintaining FABRICATE. Avoid including private player information in screenshots.

We review tester information during testing. Copies kept outside Apple’s service are deleted or anonymised within 12 months after the associated issue or test closes unless needed for a specific legal obligation or claim. Apple manages information in its service under its own policy. See TestFlight & Privacy. This is separate from local answers and scores, which the game does not upload to us.

The website has no checkout or adverts. Before introducing accounts, analytics, advertising or materially different data handling, we will update this notice and provide any required information and choices.
