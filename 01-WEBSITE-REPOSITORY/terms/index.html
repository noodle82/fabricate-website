The current game

FABRICATE! is a pass-and-play bluffing game for 3–10 players sharing one device. Players invent answers and vote for the answer they think is correct. Games have five rounds. If players tie for first place, a short reaction challenge settles the winner. Tied players gather at the screen, watch a countdown and follow the displayed move. The group selects who completed it first. Choose a safe way to carry out the displayed move, or agree a suitable alternative if it is not accessible to a player. Game scores are not changed by the tie breaker. It is intended for personal, non-commercial entertainment.

Choose Kids or Classic questions. Little Fibber Help is optional and can be enabled beside a player’s name. No age or date of birth is requested. The modes guide question difficulty; they are not an App Store content rating or a guarantee that every question suits every child.

The answer timer is optional and off by default. Background music starts off each time the game opens and plays only when switched on for that session. Sound effects are on by default; that choice is remembered.

Gameplay runs on your device. There are no player accounts, online multiplayer or FABRICATE! cloud game syncing. The website needs a connection to load its files; the information pages are bundled for reading inside the app, while external links need a connection.

Playing fairly and safely

Make up bluffs inside the game, but treat people honestly and respectfully outside it. Do not enter private, offensive or harmful information or use the game unlawfully. Adults should help younger players choose suitable settings and content.

Use nicknames where possible. Other people using or watching the shared device can see information displayed on it. Do not attempt to disrupt the service or gain unauthorised access to someone else’s device or information.

Questions and answers are entertainment, not professional advice. If you spot an error or unsuitable question, please report the wording and category through support.

Free play and Premium

Free offers 50 questions in Kids and 50 in Classic; some questions appear in both. Each new game randomly selects five question choices per round for five rounds. One question is played each round. Free website games can be replayed.

Premium is an optional one-time non-consumable in-app purchase for the separate FABRICATE iPhone and iPad app. It unlocks the full standard question bank across the modes, with 25 question choices per round, five rounds and no repeated question choices within a game. The full bank includes the Free questions. Mode eligibility still applies: not every question is available in Kids.

Premium includes additions to the standard question bank delivered through future app updates. There is no fixed update schedule or promise of a particular number of future additions. Future themed packs may be offered separately. Updates require a compatible device and internet connection; bundled questions can be played offline after entitlement is confirmed.

The UK reference price is £1.99. The local price shown by Apple before you confirm payment applies. Apple processes payment using your Apple Account. This is not a subscription and has no recurring charge. Premium includes ad-free play for this game for life, even if adverts are later added to Free. There are no adverts in the current version.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy FABRICATE Premium to restore an eligible purchase. This may require an internet connection. A purchase in BLUFFLE or UNREMARKABLE is separate and does not unlock FABRICATE. Premium purchases are not offered on the website.

Older versions may have left local data; clearing site data or removing the app can remove it.

Only your sound-effects preference is intentionally saved by the current game. Music starts off on a fresh opening even if it was switched on in a previous session. There is no cloud backup or guaranteed recovery of game progress.
