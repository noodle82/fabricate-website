Get in touch

Email support@bluffle.co.uk

If the button does not open your email app, copy the address into your usual email service. Mark Atkin handles support personally and will respond as soon as reasonably possible; no fixed response time is promised.

Include your device, operating-system or browser version, and a short description of the steps that caused the problem. You can find this page under Settings → Support & feedback. Please include the preview version shown on the game’s home screen. For a question correction, include the category and question wording.

Remove player names and other personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords or payment-card information. Younger players can ask a trusted adult for help; privacy questions are welcome directly too.

What is available now?

FABRICATE! offers free local pass-and-play for 3–10 players, five rounds, Kids and Classic questions, optional Little Fibber Help, an optional timer, music and sound effects. Fib guides the game and reacts to your fabrications.

Free has a fixed 25-question pack per mode, reshuffled into five rounds of five choices. Premium is planned at £1.99 in the UK for over 350 questions and more question choices, but buying and restoring FABRICATE Premium are not enabled in this gameplay preview.

There are no adverts, player accounts, online multiplayer or cloud game backups. Apple’s TestFlight service collects testing information separately; see the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

Why can’t I hear music or sound effects?

Music starts off each time you freshly open the game. Open Settings and switch Music on for this session. Sound effects have their own switch and start on unless you previously switched them off. Check device volume and audio settings. A browser may require a tap before allowing audio; the opening animation can continue silently. All sound recordings are included in the game files.

How do I exit?

Use Exit during play and confirm Exit game to clear the active game and player names. Keep playing returns without clearing them. Closing or reloading the app also loses the active game. Your sound-effects preference remains saved until changed or app/site data is cleared.

How do hints and the timer work?

During setup choose Kids or Classic, and enable Little Fibber? beside a player who needs a private hint. The Little Fibber Help? button appears when that player is writing. The timer is optional and off by default. If enabled, the final ten seconds appear as an on-screen countdown. No age entry is required.

How does a tie work?

Tied players gather at the screen and start the tie breaker. After the countdown, follow the displayed instruction safely. The group agrees who was first and selects that player. Agree an accessible alternative if a move does not suit someone. Earned points stay unchanged.

Can I buy or restore Premium?

Not in this gameplay preview. The Premium panel describes the planned offer but no FABRICATE payment is taken. BLUFFLE and UNREMARKABLE purchases are separate. Contact support if anything appears to ask for a real payment unexpectedly.

Does it work offline?

The installed testing app includes the Free question bank, artwork, sounds and these information pages. External websites, sending email and TestFlight services need a connection. The website needs a connection to load and does not guarantee offline reloading.

Why do I see an older version?

Wait for website deployment, then refresh or reopen the page. On Windows try Ctrl+Shift+R. Check the home footer says TESTING PREVIEW 17. TestFlight users must install the new build through TestFlight; updating the website does not change an installed app.

Can I give feedback?

Email support@bluffle.co.uk with the question wording, device, preview version and what happened. You can also use Apple’s TestFlight feedback controls. Remove private information from screenshots.

My employer blocks the website.

An organisation may block games or unfamiliar websites. Ask its IT team to review access if appropriate. We cannot change that policy.

Can you delete information about me?

We can help with support correspondence and testing information we hold. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details and your rights.
