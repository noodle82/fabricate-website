Get in touch

Email support@bluffle.co.uk

If the button does not open your email app, copy the address into your usual email service. Mark Atkin handles support personally and will respond as soon as reasonably possible; no fixed response time is promised.

Include your device, operating-system or browser version, and a short description of the steps that caused the problem. You can find this page under Settings → Support & feedback. Please include the version shown under Settings → About FABRICATE! and your device model. Do not send payment-card details. For a question correction, include the category and question wording.

Remove player names and other personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords or payment-card information. Younger players can ask a trusted adult for help; privacy questions are welcome directly too.

What is included?

FABRICATE is a local pass-and-play party game for 3–10 players, with five rounds, Kids and Classic modes, optional Little Fibber Help, an optional timer, music and sound effects.

Free has 50 questions available in each mode, with some shared between them. Each new game randomly selects five choices per round. Premium in the iPhone and iPad app unlocks the full standard question bank and 25 choices per round. Premium includes standard-bank additions through future app updates and lifetime ad-free play; future themed packs may be offered separately. The website remains Free.

There are no adverts, player accounts, online multiplayer or cloud game backups. Purchases and Restore Purchases are handled through Apple. The app does not transmit gameplay to us.

Common questions

Why can’t I hear music or sound effects?

Music starts off each time you freshly open the game. Open Settings and switch Music on for this session. Sound effects have their own switch and start on unless you previously switched them off. Check device volume and audio settings. A browser may require a tap before allowing audio; the opening animation can continue silently. All sound recordings are included in the game files.

How do I exit?

Use Exit during play and confirm Exit game to clear the active game and player names. Keep playing returns without clearing them. Closing or reloading the app also loses the active game. Your sound-effects preference remains saved until changed or app/site data is cleared.

How do hints and the timer work?

During setup choose Kids or Classic, and enable Little Fibber? beside a player who needs a private hint. The Little Fibber Help? button appears when that player is writing. The timer is optional and off by default. If enabled, the final ten seconds appear as an on-screen countdown. No age entry is required.

How does a tie work?

Tied players gather at the screen and start the tie breaker. After the countdown, follow the displayed instruction safely. The group agrees who was first and selects that player. Agree an accessible alternative if a move does not suit someone. Earned points stay unchanged.

Can I buy or restore Premium?

Yes, in the iPhone and iPad app. Open Settings → More questions? More bluff? and choose Unlock Premium or Restore Purchases. Apple handles payment and entitlement verification. The website stays Free. See Purchases and restoration below.

Does it work offline?

The installed app includes its questions, artwork, sounds and information pages. Premium questions can be played offline after entitlement has been confirmed. Buying, restoration and updates may need an internet connection. External websites, sending email and TestFlight services need a connection. The website needs a connection to load and does not guarantee offline reloading.

Why do I see an older version?

Wait for website deployment, then refresh or reopen the page. On Windows try Ctrl+Shift+R. Check the content version under Settings → About FABRICATE!. TestFlight users must install the new build through TestFlight; updating the website does not change an installed app.

Can I give feedback?

Email support@bluffle.co.uk with the question wording, device, app version and what happened. You can also use Apple’s TestFlight feedback controls. Remove private information from screenshots.

My employer blocks the website.

An organisation may block games or unfamiliar websites. Ask its IT team to review access if appropriate. We cannot change that policy.

Can you delete information about me?

We can help with support correspondence and testing information we hold. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details and your rights.

Purchases and restoration

Premium is a one-time Apple in-app purchase, with a UK reference price of £1.99. Apple’s displayed local price applies. Open Settings → More questions? More bluff? to buy or restore. Restore Purchases uses the Apple Account used for the original FABRICATE purchase; BLUFFLE and UNREMARKABLE purchases do not transfer.

If a price is unavailable, check your connection and tap Refresh shop. If Apple confirms payment but Premium is not shown, try Restore Purchases. Purchases awaiting approval do not unlock Premium until approved. On TestFlight, Apple’s purchase sheet identifies sandbox testing; follow the displayed payment information.

Premium takes effect for the next game, so a running game keeps its current question selection. Use Apple’s purchase support for refund requests. For technical help, email support@bluffle.co.uk with your device and app version. Never send card details, passwords or verification codes.
